Large format work is judged from a distance, so colour and contrast matter more than fine detail. We print at high resolution on materials chosen for where the piece will hang, whether that is a city skyline or an indoor exhibition space.

Tell us the size, the location and how long it needs to last. We will recommend the material and confirm the artwork is sharp enough at full size before anything is printed.
