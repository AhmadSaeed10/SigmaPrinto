title: How to prepare print-ready artwork
description: A short checklist for bleed, resolution and colour mode so your file prints exactly as designed.
date: 2026-09-29

## Set up the file for print

Use a PDF where possible. Add 3 mm of bleed on every edge and keep important text at least 5 mm inside the trim.

## Check resolution at final size

For close-up items such as cards and brochures, aim for 300 dpi at the printed size. Large banners viewed from a distance can be lower, but send the largest file you have.

## Convert text to outlines

Outline fonts or embed them in the PDF so nothing is replaced when the file is opened on our side.

## Expect colour to shift slightly

Screens use RGB and printers use CMYK. Bright screen blues and greens print duller. A proof shows you the real result before the full run.
